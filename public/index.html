Activity 1 · 10 min · whole room

Myth or Fact?

Read the statement aloud. Everyone stands for Fact and stays seated for Myth. Reveal, then ask one person who got it right to explain why. 10 points to each table where everyone got it right.

Activity 2 · 25 min · jigsaw teach-back

Product Passport

Each table gets one product family. Spend 10 minutes on its cards below, then teach it to the room in 3 minutes: the two products customers ask about most, one detail people usually get wrong, and a one-line pitch. The room scores each teach-back from 1 to 5 for clarity.

Activity 3 · 20 min · team quiz

BML Product Challenge

Tables take turns choosing a category and a value. The choosing table answers first. If they miss, the first other table to ring the bell can steal it. Award the points from the question window.

24 questions left
Facilitator tip: after each answer, ask "How would you say that to a customer?" A correct fact is only half the point. The other half is being able to explain it in plain words.
Activity 4 · 15 min · needs discovery

Listen for the Clue

Customers rarely say "I need a product". They mention a life event, how they are paid, something that annoys them, or a goal. Read the customer's words aloud. Tables have 45 seconds to write down the clue, the need, and the question they would ask next.

ASKOpen questions about their life, work and plans, not just the transaction.
LISTENFor four clues: life events, how they are paid, pain points, goals.
LINKConnect the need to a product, feature first and then the benefit to them.
LEADAgree a next step now, for example "Let's start it on your app together."
Activity 5 · 25 min · product matching

Match the Customer

Give each table a persona. They have 4 minutes to choose the products that fit best, including at least one cross-sell, and to write the first question they would ask. One table presents. Tap their picks below, then press Check.

Picked and recommendedRecommended, missedAlso reasonableAsk the table why
Activity 6 · 15 min · cross-selling in routine service

Spot the Opportunity

A routine request arrives at the counter. Tables get 2 minutes to list every genuine opportunity hidden in it. Reveal the opportunities one at a time. Each table earns the points shown for every one on their list.

Activity 7 · 10 min · confidence round

Spin & Explain

One volunteer per table. Spin, then explain the product to an imaginary customer in 30 seconds, using Feature, Advantage and Benefit. The room votes with thumbs up. A clear pitch earns 30 points.

Close · 5 min

From knowing to doing

Announce the winning table. Then ask everyone to write three commitments on a sticky note and post it on the wall on their way out. Collect the notes for the Day 2 sales simulation.

One productI will explain more confidently tomorrow, and the one line I will use.
One questionI will ask every customer this week to uncover a need.
One customerI will move to self-service on the app, with me beside them the first time.
Confidence check

Ask the same question at the start and at the end: "On a scale of 1 to 5, how confident are you explaining BML's financing products to a customer?" Count hands for each number and write both counts on the flipchart. The change in the room is your quickest measure of whether the session worked.
